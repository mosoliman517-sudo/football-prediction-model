How to read this in 20 seconds
  • A call is the result the model picks for a match: Home win, Draw or Away win. A green tick means it was right.
  • The percentages are the model's honest chances. 70% means it should be right about 7 times in 10.
  • Football is noisy. Even bookmakers get only about half of results right, so about 50% is a good score, not a bad one.
  • Never seen these matches means it was trained on 2014 to 2024 only, so every number here is a real test.

How to read this

  • The model never saw these matches. It trained on 2014-15 to 2023-24 and is replaying seasons after that. Every number here is real out-of-sample output.
  • Percentages are honest odds; the playing style only picks the call. The odds are an even blend of the Random Forest and the Poisson goal model, which beat both parts on log loss and match the real Home/Draw/Away rates. A playing style decides which outcome gets called, so a Draw call can sit next to a 30% Draw chance, on purpose.
  • Playing styles are decision rules, not different models. Balanced is tuned so Home wins, Draws and Away wins are each found about as often as possible, which costs some overall accuracy. Max Accuracy ignores balance and suppresses Draw, which gives the most results right. The three coloured numbers on each card are the share of real Home wins, Draws and Away wins that style found on the 760 held-out matches. The decision can be made from our blended model (the default, tuned in step 10 of the pipeline), from the Random Forest alone, or from the model plus bookmaker odds.
  • The bookmaker line is a yardstick, not an input, except in the one opt-in mode that says so. "Model + bookmaker odds" stacks our blend with the bookmaker market's average closing odds, which is real information but carries what the market knows (lineups, injuries, news), so it is labelled market-informed. Everything else never sees an odd.
  • Two kinds of simulation, kept apart on purpose. Replays and next-matchday forecasts are always built from real past results only. The blind rest-of-season simulation is the one place predicted results feed predictions: after each simulated match, Elo, form and the table update with the simulated result.
  • The match lab forecasts unplayed games from each team's latest state. It reads form and Elo from each club's most recent match, the real current table for position and points, and head-to-head history. Kickoff time, rest days and European fixtures are unknown for a game that hasn't been scheduled in the data, so they take neutral values. Saved calls are kept in your own private storage for this page.
  • Data: match results and shots from football-data.co.uk, expected goals from Understat, squad values, transfers and managers from a Kaggle copy of Transfermarkt. Matchdays are every 10 matches in date order.
