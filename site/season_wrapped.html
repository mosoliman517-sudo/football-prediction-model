Pick any home and away team. If that match has been played, you see the model's call and its simulated scorelines next to the real result. If it hasn't, you get a forecast you can simulate and save. When the real result shows up in the data, your saved call is scored.

How to read this

  • The model never saw these matches. It trained on 2014-15 to 2023-24 and is replaying seasons after that. Every number here is real out-of-sample output.
  • Profiles are decision rules, not different models. The Random Forest produces three probabilities per match. A profile nudges them before picking one: Balanced and Target boost Draw and Away so those outcomes get called more often, Deadkill leaves the probabilities alone except to suppress Draw, which gives the best raw accuracy.
  • The bookmaker line is a yardstick, not an input. It shows how often simply picking Bet365's pre-match favourite would have been right. The model never uses odds.
  • The match lab forecasts unplayed games from each team's latest state. It reads form and Elo from each club's most recent match, the real current table for position and points, and head-to-head history. Kickoff time, rest days and European fixtures are unknown for a game that hasn't been scheduled in the data, so they take neutral values. Saved calls are kept in your own private storage for this page.
  • Data: match results and shots from football-data.co.uk, expected goals from Understat, squad values, transfers and managers from a Kaggle copy of Transfermarkt. Rounds are every 10 matches in date order.
