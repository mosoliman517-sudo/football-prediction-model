Can you out-call a model?

Pick a league. Predict real matches the model has never seen, then watch the call and the result land. Every league has its own look, its own sounds and the same honest scoring.

Predictions come from a model that only ever saw earlier seasons (walk-forward), so every match you play was unseen. Bookmaker comparison uses average closing odds with the margin removed. Club and league names belong to their owners. The coloured discs and emblems are original drawings, not official crests or logos, and no third-party artwork or photographs are used. This is an independent student project, not affiliated with any league, club or player. Results and odds come from football-data.co.uk.
